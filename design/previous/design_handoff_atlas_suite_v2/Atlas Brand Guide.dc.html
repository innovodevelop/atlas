Brand Guidelines

Atlas

A calm, warm personal-intelligence brand. Paper surfaces, ink type, one signal color. These are the essential assets and the rules that keep them consistent.

01

Wordmark

The logo is type-only — no icon in the lockup. Two are in use: L2 is the product wordmark (dashboard, in-app chrome); L1 is the brand wordmark (marketing, documents, headers). L3–L6 are archived explorations.

Clear space

Keep one cap-height of clear space on all sides of the wordmark.

Minimum size

Never smaller than 20px tall on screen, 8mm in print.

Color

Ink on paper, paper on ink. Signal Orange only on the accent glyph.

Don't

No icon lockups, no stretching, no gradients, no drop shadows.

03

Color

Warm neutrals do 90% of the work. Orange is the only signal color — one accent per view.

Whites stay warm — keep saturation subtle, never pure #FFF for large surfaces. Green and Red are reserved for status (gains/losses, success/error). Atlas Blue is a supporting data color, never a CTA.
04

Typography

Two families, three weights. Numerals are always tabular.

05

Shape & surface

Soft radii, hairline borders, no card shadows — elevation comes from a −3px hover lift and border shifts.

10 — inputs

Borders

Always 1px, #E2DED7. Hover darkens to #B6B1AA.

Texture

Fixed grain overlay at 40% multiply over background glows.

Glow

Warm blurred blobs (orange, cream, a hint of blue) behind content only.

06

Iconography

Lucide, 16px, 2px stroke, ink — contained in 34–38px sand boxes. Orange fill only for the active state.

07
