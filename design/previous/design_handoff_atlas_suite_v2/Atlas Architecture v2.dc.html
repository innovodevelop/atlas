The part worth being clear about

Your memories stay on this Mac. The requests built from them do not.

Atlas keeps everything it knows about you in a single file on your own disk. No copy is uploaded, no account holds it, and deleting it deletes it.

But to answer you, it has to send your question somewhere — and it sends the relevant memories along with it, because that is what makes the answer feel like yours.

So the file never travels. Sentences from it do, a handful at a time, every time you ask something. That's the honest shape of it.

Search by meaning

Ask about "the pitch" and it finds the note that says "Innovo deck". The maths that makes that work runs locally, so your documents are never indexed by anyone else.

On-device embeddings, sqlite-vec ANN

Listening for its name

The microphone is open, and nothing it hears goes anywhere until you say the wake phrase. That check happens here, on a model small enough to run constantly.

openWakeWord, ONNX runtime

Knowing when you've stopped

Atlas waits for a real pause rather than a fixed timer, and it stops talking the moment you start. Both need to react in milliseconds, which rules out a round trip.

Silero VAD, barge-in on the local gateway

How it remembers

Three kinds of memory

They behave differently on purpose. Only the third one lasts.

01

This conversation

What you've said in the last few minutes. Held in the moment, gone when the conversation ends.

02

What it fetched

A handful of memories pulled in because they seemed relevant to what you just asked. Assembled fresh each turn, then discarded.

03

What it keeps

Facts filed under a category — who you are, your work, the people around you, what you like — each with a sense of how much it matters. This is the file on your disk.

How it learns

Learning happens after the fact

Nothing about you is trained into a model. Learning here means writing things down and reading them back.

1

Notice

Something you said looks worth keeping

2

File it

Given a category and a weight, then written to disk

3

Reconcile

If it contradicts something older, the older one is superseded, not silently overwritten

4

Recall

Next time it's relevant, it comes back into the request

Overnight, the same loop runs without you: summarising the day, following up on research you asked for. That's the work that goes to Bedrock in Europe.
