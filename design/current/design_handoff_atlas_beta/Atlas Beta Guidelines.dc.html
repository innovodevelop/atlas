02

Shape & space

Radius gets smaller as you nest deeper. The gap between surfaces is always 12px, and page padding is 26px. Rows take 9–12px vertical padding when they sit on their own fill, or 15–20px when they sit on the parent's fill.

04

Widget grid

Six columns with a 12px gap, packed dense under the band. Cards are #fffdfa, radius 24 and padding 20–22. A card that gets more space fills it with more content (a chart, stats or a side list), never with empty padding. Clicking a card opens its full view.

06

Controls

Every button, input and textarea sets border:none. Buttons are always pills, and hovering only changes the fill. These are all live, so try them.

Buttons

Heights are 36 for actions, 34 for chips and 28 for actions inside rows. Use one primary button per region.

07

Veil sphere

{{ sphereLabel }}

Where it appears: in the band (every screen), the Dock avatar, the conversation drawer and the editor veil. It's the same Veil motion on every surface. Tide and Spiral stay on the Quick Start page only.

Density: 20k particles in a band, 4k at mini size. Set the background to the fill the sphere sits on.

Interaction: the inner 72% of the sphere is the hit area and opens the conversation drawer.

Transitions: states ease over about 1 second (spring settle). Never jump-cut between states.
