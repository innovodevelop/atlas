The part worth being clear about

Your memories stay on this Mac. The requests built from them do not.

Atlas keeps everything it knows about you in a single file on your own disk. No copy is uploaded, no account holds it, and deleting it deletes it.

But to answer you, it has to send your question somewhere, and it sends the relevant memories along with it. That's what makes the answer feel like yours.

So the file never travels. Sentences from it do, a handful at a time, every time you ask something. That's the honest shape of it.
