Internal reference

The Atlas
design system

One language for every Atlas surface — the tokens, type, components, motion and light that make the product feel calm, warm and alive. Build from these; don't reinvent them.

01

A warm paper base with a single confident accent. Blue is Atlas's voice — used sparingly for the live, active and interactive. Orange is a secondary accent for warmth and highlights; green and red are reserved for status and markets; signal blue belongs to the atmosphere and network visualisations.

06

The light language

Atlas's signature. State is expressed as light, not chrome. The orb breathes when idle, sharpens when listening, blooms when speaking. Weather and system health render as atmosphere — warm glow for good, cool blue for calm, cloud and rain for degraded. Surfaces are frosted so this light shows faintly through the whole product.

Orb · listening → speaking

Rules of the light:

Atlas blue — live, active, interactive Warm orange — secondary accent, highlights, energy Green — healthy, resolved, gains Grey haze / rain — degraded, overcast, idle

Motion is slow and organic — drift, breathe, bloom. Never blink, jitter or strobe. Light additive-blends; it never uses hard edges.

10

Voice & tone

Atlas speaks like a capable, unhurried chief of staff. Warm, brief, and always a step ahead — never chirpy, never robotic.

"Friday's 9:40 to Paris is the strongest option — fares dip midweek and rain clears by the weekend."

Leads with the answer. Shows the reasoning briefly. No filler, no hedging.

1

Answer first

The recommendation up front; supporting detail after.

2

Warm, not cute

Human and calm. No exclamation marks or emoji.

3

Show the work

Name the sources it checked so trust is earned.

11

Accessibility

Calm should never mean unclear. Atlas targets WCAG 2.1 AA across every surface.

Contrast & targets

Body textInk on Paper — 13.6:1 (AAA)
SecondaryInk-2 on Paper — 4.9:1 (AA)
Accent textUse #2A4FD0 on Paper for blue text (5.6:1); reserve #3461F2 for fills/icons.
Hit target≥ 44×44px, even when the glyph is smaller.

Interaction & motion

✓Visible focus ring on every control — 3px accent glow (never remove outline without a replacement).
✓Never signal by color alone — pair gain/loss and status with a sign, icon or label.
✓Honour prefers-reduced-motion — freeze drift, breathe and stream; keep instant state.
✓Icon-only buttons carry an aria-label; live regions announce Atlas replies.
12

Do & don't

Do

Use orange only for what is live, active or interactive.
Let surfaces be frosted so the atmosphere reads through.
Express state as light — glow, bloom, drift.
Keep motion slow and organic; stagger entrances.
Lead copy with the answer.

Don't

Flood the UI with blue — one confident accent; orange only as a rare secondary.
Use hard grey drop shadows or heavy borders.
Blink, strobe or jitter — light never flickers.
Reach for emoji or exclamation marks in Atlas's voice.
Introduce new fonts, radii or one-off colors.
